# Feedback and Issues

Thanks for taking the time to report a bug or suggest an idea for DailyRation.

## Feedback terms

By submitting any feedback, suggestion, feature request, idea, or other content
through this issue tracker or any other channel, you agree that the developer may
use it for any purpose - including building it into DailyRation - freely, without
restriction, compensation, attribution, or obligation to you, and you waive any
claim of ownership over it. Please submit only your own ideas, not anyone else's
confidential or proprietary information.

(For the curious: ideas on their own generally aren't owned by anyone under
copyright or patent law. This note just makes the expectation clear, so a good
suggestion can simply get built.)

## Reporting a bug

Open an issue and include:

- What you did
- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened
- Your device and OS version (e.g. iPhone 15, iOS 17.4; or Mac, macOS 14.4)
